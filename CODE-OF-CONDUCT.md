# Code of Conduct

**A professional, production-focused engineering environment.**

---

## Our Commitment

The Kafka Engineering Lab is a professional, production-focused engineering environment.

We are committed to creating a respectful, serious, and technically rigorous community where contributors can collaborate safely and productively.

Participation in this project — whether through issues, discussions, pull requests, or reviews — requires adherence to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- ✅ Be respectful and professional
- ✅ Focus discussions on technical merit
- ✅ Provide constructive feedback
- ✅ Accept review comments without hostility
- ✅ Assume good intent
- ✅ Communicate clearly and thoughtfully
- ✅ Support reproducibility and correctness

> 💡 **Disagreement is acceptable. Disrespect is not.**

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:

- ❌ Personal attacks or insults
- ❌ Harassment or intimidation
- ❌ Discriminatory language
- ❌ Dismissing contributors without technical reasoning
- ❌ Derailing discussions away from technical topics
- ❌ Public shaming
- ❌ Hostile responses to review feedback

> ⚠️ **This repository is a professional engineering space — not a social battleground.**

---

## Technical Disagreements

Distributed systems discussions can become intense.

**When disagreements arise:**

- Provide evidence
- Provide documentation
- Provide production reasoning
- Avoid emotional escalation
- Keep arguments focused on architecture and correctness

**If consensus cannot be reached,** maintainers will make the final decision in the interest of:

- Stability
- Reproducibility
- Long-term maintainability

---

## Enforcement

**Maintainers may:**

- Issue warnings
- Request edits to comments
- Lock threads
- Remove inappropriate content
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation

> ⚖️ **Enforcement decisions prioritize project health and community safety.**

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

- Report it privately to a maintainer
- Provide context and evidence if possible

**Reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully.**
